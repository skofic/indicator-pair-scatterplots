Phase 3 — display examples

Pair P1 (annual mean temperature × annual precipitation), Fagus sylvatica, its 530 units, present and future, from the real collections (work/examples/data/, written by sources/export/pair.js).

  1. Scatterplot — D3 + Canvas (the chosen library): product / scenario / period, densities, 95 % envelope, present → future arrows, unit search and selection, box zoom.
  2. Map — OpenLayers: Atlas presence COG read directly in the browser, EU-Trees4F current and 2095 layers, chorology outline, units; click a location to read its values; draw a point, box or polygon.
  3. Map ↔ scatterplot: both panes on one page; what is drawn on the map is shown in the scatterplot (teal), present → selected period.
