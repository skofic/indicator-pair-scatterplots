Phase 3 — display examples

The panes read their data from the panes' Foxx service in GeoService (/panes, see service.js; ?api= points elsewhere) and their texts from the data dictionary (metadata/ui, see dictionary.js; ?lang= switches the language once the terms are translated). The pane state is keyed by dictionary terms, so any state key works as a URL parameter. Every EU-Forest species, every unit, three climate sources with their futures, five indicator pairs. The rasters come from this repository's maps/ folder, served by examples/serve.mjs.

  1. Scatterplot — D3 + Canvas: source / scenario / period, X and Y indicator with paired suggestions, densities, 95 % envelope, present → future arrows, the EU-Trees4F suitability classes, unit search and selection, box zoom.
  2. Map — OpenLayers: Atlas presence COG read directly in the browser, EU-Trees4F current and 2095 layers, chorology outline, units; click a location to read its values; draw a point, box or polygon.
  3. Map ↔ scatterplot: both panes on one page with shared controls (species, country, unit, restricted, flag threshold); side by side or stacked following the window's shape; what is drawn on the map is shown in the scatterplot (teal), present → selected period; clearing on either pane clears both.
